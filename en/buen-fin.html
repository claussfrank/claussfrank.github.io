Best Buy México

+3,905% revenue with a landing page organized by product category

As my first campaign entirely on my own, I designed and built a landing page organized by product category for Buen Fin 2019 — Mexico's equivalent of Black Friday. Working with Marketing, I identified two user profiles — people who already knew what they were looking for, and people who just wanted to catch a deal — and designed the landing page to serve both at once.

Domain
E-commerce (consumer electronics)
Duration
1-2 months planning + 2 weeks build
Team
Designer and developer (solo)
Role
End-to-end design and development

Two user profiles, one landing page

The previous landing page had no submenu for finding Buen Fin promotions by category. Together with Marketing, we identified two profiles: people who already knew which product they wanted and needed to find it quickly by category, and people with no specific purchase in mind who just wanted to take advantage of the deals.

For the second profile I designed a carousel of the biggest discounts, on the hypothesis — not empirically proven, something I had read at the time — that seeing more discounted products creates excitement and encourages impulse buying.

Designing inside a proprietary platform

I had to stay within the campaign's already established visual guidelines. Because it was a proprietary platform, JavaScript could not be added freely for security reasons, so I worked with the pre-approved widgets that fit best, reusing classes and structure that already existed on the site.

What was kept

The campaign's overall approach showed products by discount. I did not abandon it entirely: I kept a best-deals carousel for the profile that was only looking for promotions.

The trade-off

Neither profile had to be sacrificed for the other — the landing page served both at once. If anything, the trade-off was time: reusing already-approved widgets instead of proposing something visually more ambitious.

Outcome

+3,905% revenue compared to the previous campaign. Thanks to the SEO investment made between 2018 and 2019, organic traffic to the landing page grew 98% year over year, generating $1,953,884 MXN in purchases (out of $62,900,000 MXN total revenue on the landing page). According to the Hotjar heatmap (a 2,000-user sample), 44% clicked on the promotions-by-price section.

I no longer have the previous campaign's revenue figure (the baseline for the +3,905% calculation) — only the final comparative result that was presented at the time.

What I learned

It was my first campaign entirely on my own — my manager resigned right before the project started, and I was the only person who knew the platform. There were days without much sleep, but seeing the result was deeply satisfying. The following years were easier: I developed better maturity in managing timelines. In fact, it was at Best Buy that I met Sofi, who years later would be my teammate on the Autocobro usability tests.
