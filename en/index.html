About me

You have probably seen plenty of portfolios today. I would rather not repeat what they all say, so here it is straight: I am Clau, a UX/UI designer who also understands the technology she works with. If you would rather go straight to the work, here are my projects. But if you stay a moment, let me tell you how I think about what I do.

Every project is a different expedition. Some call for more analysis and patience; others, because of the timeline, call for persuasion — or for going out to find the truth yourself when the official channel is not enough. What never changes is the destination: that the person using what I design solves their problem through technology, with no unnecessary burden and no avoidable errors, and that this works for the business too.

To keep getting to know me, picture yourself at an outdoor camp. Every trail you see below is an expedition I have already walked.

Soft Skills

Whatever the destination, there are tools I always carry in my backpack. These are the ones I use most:

Boots:

Like a good pair of shoes, teamwork is what lets you move forward on solid ground. For me, teamwork rests on respect, listening constantly and learning continuously. I am always open to new ideas and to learning from the people around me.

Map:

A good map helps you read every aspect of the route and of the teams walking it toward a shared goal. It is not only about knowing where you want to arrive, but also about understanding where you have already been and what the next step is. In my experience, the journey matters as much as the destination.

Lantern:

Learning something new every day is like lighting a lamp in the dark. It guides us, it shows us the path ahead, and when we get it wrong, it helps us find a new route.

Rope:

Resilience is like a firm rope that holds you when you fall. Knowing that even if you stumble you can get back up and keep going is essential. It does not matter how many times you fall — what matters is always carrying on.

Compass:

Knowing what each member of the expedition does is what lets you understand how to support them better. Knowing your own path is not enough — understanding everyone else's is what keeps the team moving in the same direction.
